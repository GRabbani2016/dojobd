Why Digital Certificate & ID Verify Matters for Modern Dojos

Whether a student is transferring academies, applying for school sports credits, or entering an inter-club championship, verifying rank authenticity matters. dojo.bd includes a public Certificate & ID Verification tool on your website that validates unique registration numbers and displays verified rank credentials.

Key Capabilities & Built-In Tools

Unique Credential IDs

Assign tamper-evident registration numbers to every student ID card and belt promotion certificate.

Public Verification Widget

Visitors enter a certificate or student ID code directly on your website to confirm authenticity.

Detailed Rank Output

Displays verified student name, belt rank, issue date, issuing dojo/federation, and active status.

Federation-Ready Standards

Ideal for national associations and multi-branch academies issuing official Dan & Kyu certificates.
