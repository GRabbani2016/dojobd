Why Event & Tournament Registration Matters for Modern Dojos

Organizing a karate tournament or guest-sensei seminar involves coordinating weight categories, age divisions, entry deadlines, and club rosters. dojo.bd's Event & Tournament Registration module lets you publish event notices, rules, and schedules while collecting participant entries online.

Event Landing Pages

Division & Category Selection

Notice Board & Announcements
